## 🏦 Customer Attrition Analysis and Prediction in the Banking Sector using Machine Learning Classifiers

Customer attrition, also known as customer churn, is an important concern in the banking sector as losing existing customers can affect customer relationships and long-term profitability. This project aims to **analyse customer characteristics and predict the likelihood of customer attrition using Machine Learning techniques**.

The analysis uses a dataset containing **10,000 banking customer records**, including information such as credit score, geography, gender, age, tenure, account balance, number of products, credit card ownership, activity status, and estimated salary. By applying **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), feature engineering, and Machine Learning classification**, the project explores patterns associated with customer churn and develops predictive models to identify customers who may be at risk of leaving the bank.
